# TrimSwipe - 25-Second App Store Preview Screenplay

## Creative direction

- Format: portrait iPhone App Preview, exactly 25.0 seconds, 30 fps.
- Language: English throughout the app and overlays.
- Focus: TrimSwipe only. Do not show the other games.
- Visual tone: calm, clean, warm, and premium. Use the real app interface and licensed demo photos with no personal information.
- Motion: preserve the app's native spring movement for swipes. Between screens, use restrained 8-10 frame dissolves or match-cuts; no hard cuts, fake UI, device mockups, or flashy transitions.
- Sound: no voiceover. Use one light, modern instrumental bed with soft tactile accents for taps and swipes. The story must remain fully understandable while muted.

## Capture and edit notes

1. Set the app language to **English** before recording and keep all system prompts out of the footage.
2. Prepare a clean demo photo library using only original or properly licensed images. Avoid faces, notifications, names, locations, and other personal information.
3. Start with populated, internally consistent demo stats. The recap and Stats figures should agree and should come from real in-app actions.
4. Record the app at native resolution. Add only simple touch indicators and the short captions above.
5. Keep important copy away from the Dynamic Island, status bar, bottom navigation, and App Store crop-safe edges.
6. Select a poster frame around **00:05.0**, when the Keep swipe and core interaction are immediately understandable.
7. Export an App Store master as H.264 MP4, **886 x 1920 portrait**, 30 fps, progressive, 10-12 Mbps, with stereo 256 kbps AAC audio at 48 kHz. Keep the finished file below 500 MB.
